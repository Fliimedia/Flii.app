Grant access to Flii Media

LinkedIn: Page access ▶

You add a Flii Media team member as an admin on your LinkedIn Company Page. You do this as a Super admin of the Page.

Team member to add: Sten Bossong

  1. Sign in to LinkedIn as a Super admin of your Company Page and open your Page super admin view.
  2. Click Settings in the left menu, then select Manage admins.
  3. Open the Page admins tab.
  4. Click Add admin.
  5. In Search for a member, type the name of the Flii team member above. If they do not appear, ask them to follow your Page first, then try again.
  6. Select their name from the list.
  7. Set the role to Super admin.
  8. Click Save.

Meta: Page and pixel access ▶

You grant Flii Media partner access to your Facebook Page and your pixel (now called a dataset), into Flii's Business Portfolio.

Flii Media Business Portfolio ID: 169437185030360

  1. Go to business.facebook.com/settings and select the correct Business Portfolio (top left).
  2. In the left menu, under Users, click Partners.
  3. Click Add, then choose Give a partner access to your assets.
  4. Enter Flii Media's Business Portfolio ID (above) and click Next.
  5. Under Pages, select your Page and set the permission to Full control.
  6. Under Datasets (formerly Pixels), select your pixel and set the permission to Full control.
  7. Click Assign assets (or Save changes).
  • If the pixel is not listed in the partner step, assign it separately: Data sources > Datasets > your dataset > Partners > Assign partner, then enter the same Business Portfolio ID and set Full control.
If a Page or dataset does not appear in the list, it is not connected to your Business Portfolio yet. Add it first under Accounts > Pages or Data sources > Datasets, then repeat the step.
